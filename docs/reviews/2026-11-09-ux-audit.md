# UX audit and redesign concept: Ruhestandsplanung workspace

- **Brief (owner, verbatim):** „überarbeite das Design so, dass die Bedienung noch viel intuitiver und eleganter wird, wobei wir immer das Ergebnis im Blick behalten wollen."
- **Audited:** `/de/simulation` (plus landing and `/de/setup`) at commit `f1beaf3`, dev server, headless Chromium, 9 Nov 2026.
- **Viewports:** 1366×890 (primary, iPad Pro 12.9" landscape), 1440×900, 1920×1080, 1024×768, 390×844.
- **Plan used:** the example plan plus three flows added through the panel, as the brief asked: pension „ÄVLB Brandenburg (S2: ab 2034)" 1.850 €/Monat ab 63, one-off „CCDRD Kapitaloption brutto (S2, Jan 2033)" 125.000 € mit 61, expense „Private Krankenversicherung inkl. Pflegeversicherung" 780 €/Monat ab 60. That gives 16 flows (5 incomes and pensions, 11 expenses), 96,2 % success, 1,85 Mio € median end assets.
- **Numbers** are written as in the German UI: 6.580 px, 7,4 screens, 96,2 %.
- **Evidence:** 213 screenshots in [`shots/`](shots-not-in-repo/), step logs in `log-tasks-*.txt`, per-viewport measurements in `tour-*.json` and `measure-1366.json`, the Playwright scripts in `scripts/`.

**Limits of this audit.** The UI was audited as of `f1beaf3`; a commit that landed on the branch during the audit (`bab97ce`) only touches `.gitignore` and `next.config.ts`. Chromium only, so nothing here is verified in Safari. Touch was emulated at 1024 and 390 only; the 1366 runs used a mouse, so iPad touch findings rest on measured target sizes, not on touch runs. Timings are from a dev build. No real user was observed: "first-time user" means "knows nothing but what the screen says".

---

## The finding in one paragraph

The app keeps two numbers in view, not the result. The sticky bar shows the success rate and the median end assets on a 57 px strip; everything that explains them (the fan chart, "reicht bis", spending) lives in the first of five sections on a page that is 7,4 screens long at 1366×890. The fan chart is on screen for 17 % of the scroll range and fully visible for 5 %. The controls that change the result sit in five different places, and the two the owner uses most for scenarios (quick levers and "Unsichere Posten") are 4.200–5.000 px below the chart. When a number moves, the page says by how much ("+1,2 Pkt.") but never shows the old picture next to the new one, and never lists what was changed. The visual system itself is disciplined and worth keeping; the problem is structure, duplication and legibility of cause and effect.

**Scorecard.** Of the 11 paths that change the plan (1a–4b, 6, 7's „Übernehmen", 11), the fan chart was in view at the moment of the change in **two** (1a and 4b), and both start from a life-stage caption that does not look clickable. „Reicht bis" was in view in the same two. A first-time user takes the labelled routes (rail → Annahmen, rail → Stellschrauben) and gets the bar only.

**At 1024 and 390** the same paths end worse: the overlay covers the right half of the chart and dims the rest ([`1024-task1a-retirement-64.png`](shots-not-in-repo/1024-task1a-retirement-64.png)); the phone sheet replaces everything with „Erfolg 97,4 % +1,2 Pkt." ([`390-task1a-retirement-64.png`](shots-not-in-repo/390-task1a-retirement-64.png)). On the phone, Entnahme is one column, so choosing a rule shows neither stats nor corridor ([`390-task6-entnahme-fixed.png`](shots-not-in-repo/390-task6-entnahme-fixed.png)).

**First-time versus returning.** A first-time user reads the rail as the map and follows it top to bottom: Ergebnis (look), Annahmen (four cards, each one more click), Geldfluss, Entnahme, Stellschrauben. The words do not say that Annahmen and Stellschrauben edit the same values, or that Entnahme is also an input. A returning user learns two habits: go to Stellschrauben for sliders and switches, and watch the bar. Neither habit brings the chart along.

The system is tidy: seven type tokens, weights 400/500/600, one radius (4 px), 1 px hairlines, a single accent. That should stay. The noise comes from somewhere else:

1. **Small grey text carries most of the page.** On Ergebnis three quarters of the characters are muted grey and 43 % are 12 px; on Entnahme two thirds are 12 px. The page holds 1.355 words before any panel opens (flows panel 298 more, market panel 313). At arm's length on an iPad the explanations compete with the numbers they explain.
2. **Stray sizes.** 10,4 px, 11 px and 13 px appear outside the scale (panel hints, chart ticks, flow rows).
3. **Boxes return where controls gather.** Stellschrauben and the flows panel reach 7–9 distinct border treatments (cards, chips, dashed template tiles, dashed „Erweiterte Optionen", segmented buttons, table frame, switch tracks).
4. **Repetition.** The success rate appears up to four times on the page (bar, verdict, Entnahme stat, „Basis 96,2 %"), the end assets twice in two formats (1,85 Mio € and 1.854.636 €). On the phone both pairs share the first screen.
5. **At 1920** 36–44 % of the viewport is empty margin while the chart is capped at 990 px ([`1920-ergebnis-top-margins.png`](shots-not-in-repo/1920-ergebnis-top-margins.png)).

Dark mode holds up; no separate findings ([`1366-dark-panel-flows.png`](shots-not-in-repo/1366-dark-panel-flows.png), [`390-dark-ergebnis-top.png`](shots-not-in-repo/390-dark-ergebnis-top.png)).

# Part B: Redesign concepts

All three concepts share one new ingredient, because the audit shows it is missing whatever the layout: **the saved plan stays on the chart as a ghost** (dashed median, thin outline of the band) while the draft is drawn on top, on a y-scale that holds still until you save or discard. Every edit then shows the old and the new future, not just a number. The concepts differ in *where the chart lives while you edit* and *how you edit*.

Wireframes use the audit plan after one change (Ruhestart 62 → 64: 97,4 %, +1,2 Punkte, 2,21 Mio €). Headline figures are measured; the smaller figures inside the wireframes are placeholders.

## Concept A: „Leinwand und Steuerspalte" (result canvas beside one control column)

**Idea.** Turn the page inside out. Today the page is where you read and a panel slides in where you edit. In A the two are permanent neighbours. On the left is the result canvas: its head (numbers and fan chart with ghost) is the first thing on it, and when you scroll down into Geldfluss or Entnahme a slim dock with a mini chart takes its place, so some form of the picture is always there. On the right is one control column that holds every input exactly once, in five groups: the four panels of today plus Entnahme. The rail goes; its jobs are done by three anchors in the canvas and five group headers in the column. The Annahmen cards become the closed state of the groups. The Stellschrauben sliders go, because the column *is* the lever board; their impact lists stay as a section „Wirkung". No drag-and-drop is needed for A to work.

# Recommendation: A as the frame, C's dock inside it, B's direct manipulation in doses

Build **concept A**. Use **C's dock** as the collapsed form of A's head and as the result on tablet and phone; it is the same component in a second size. Take from **B** only the parts that pay for themselves on this plan: the ghost (already shared), a draggable Ruhestart marker on the chart, the link from a year on the chart to Geldfluss, and a thin plan lane under the chart that starts read-only.

Why not B as a whole: with 16 flows the timeline has to fold the items the owner edits most, it cannot edit amounts, and it fails on the phone. Why not C alone: it answers „im Blick" and leaves „intuitiver und eleganter" untouched, because every duplicate input stays. A removes the duplication and the long walk, and it grows out of code that exists: the docked panel is already a third grid column with its own scroll, and the Entnahme section is already split into cause and outcome.

One decision in A is yours to confirm before building: **the rail and the Stellschrauben sliders go.** The rail's five targets become three anchors and five group headers; the sliders disappear because every one of them duplicates a field in the column. If you want to keep a compact „try quickly" row, it can be a saved set of pinned controls at the top of the column, but it would be the second home for those values again.

The y-scale covers both and holds still while the plan is dirty; it re-fits on save and discard. Markers for Ruhestart and each pension start on the axis. **Dock** (96 px, full canvas width, pinned under the bar): success rate 24/600 with delta chip · end assets 16/600 with delta · „bis 92+" · mini chart 240×64 (median, outer band, ghost median, Ruhestart tick) · „1 Änderung ▾" (opens the chip list) · run status. Widths are reserved (`min-inline-size` per value and for the delta slot), so nothing shifts when a delta appears; that also fixes F6.

Save and discard stay in the bar with their test ids. The bar reads, left to right: plan menu · draft state, „Verwerfen", „Speichern ▾" · space · „Nominal │ Heutige €" · PDF · Menü.

## R3. The control column

Sticky under the bar, full remaining height, own scroll, `overscroll-behavior: contain`: the mechanics of today's docked panel.

- **Five groups:** Zeit · Vermögen & Sparen · Einnahmen & Ausgaben · Entnahme · Markt & Steuern. A header is 56 px: chevron, title (16/600), and at the right the key figure the Annahmen card shows today (14 px, muted); a dot marks a group with unsaved changes. Headers stick to the top of the column while their group scrolls. Several groups can be open; „Zeit" and „Einnahmen & Ausgaben" are open by default.
- **Rows are 44 px** or taller. A flow row reads: switch · name (two lines at most) · amount with its unit („1.850 €/Mon.") · measured effect when available („ohne −16,2") · ⋯. A tap on the row opens period, tax and delete in place. From phase 2 the amount is edited in place and is live; the row's own „Speichern" goes.
- **„+ Posten"** sits at the top of the group and opens the composer right there. The new row scrolls into view and flashes.
- The panel's timeline („Wann welcher Posten gilt") leaves the column; from phase 2 the plan lane under the chart shows the same thing on the chart's axis.
- Footer: „Geführte Einrichtung" and „Auf Standard zurücksetzen".

## R4. Interaction principles

1. **One home per value.** Every input exists once, in the column. A time value may also have a handle on the chart; that is the same control drawn a second time, and both move together.
2. **One control per kind of value.** Today there are nine mechanisms; five are enough.

   | Kind | Control | Notes |
   |---|---|---|
   | Age | Stepper field `[−] 64 [+]` | Phase 2 adds the marker on the chart axis. |
   | Amount | Number field with € and unit, thousands grouping, arrow keys step | Edited in place in lists. |
   | Rate or percentage | Slider with plan tick, exact value field at the right, ↺ | One slider look. |
   | On or off | Switch | 44×44 px hit area. |
   | Choice (rule, market model, presets) | Radio list or segmented control | Presets fill the sliders; they are not a second value. |

3. **Every edit is live and visibly lands.** Numbers tween over 240 ms (existing `AnimatedNumber`), the fan moves from the old shape to the new over 280 ms while the ghost stays put, the delta chips cross-fade. Nothing else on the screen moves.
4. **Undo is always one click.** ↺ on a changed control (its tick shows the saved value), ✕ on a change chip, „Verwerfen" for everything with the existing undo toast. Phase 3 adds Cmd/Ctrl+Z over an in-memory list of draft patches.
5. **Picture first, number second, words third.** Ghost, then delta, then the chip that names the cause.
6. **One baseline per place.** The head and dock always compare with the saved plan. Effects in rows („ohne −16,2") compare with the current draft and say so in words, never with the same chip.
7. **Views are linked.** A year picked on the chart sets the Geldfluss year. A Sankey item flashes its row in the column (existing `focusField`).
8. **Editing is never modal.** Dialogs are for naming and for destructive confirmations only.
9. **Small effects stay quiet.** A delta under 0,5 points is shown as „±0", in neutral colour.

## R5. Visual language

- **Type: five sizes.** 12 (units, axis ticks, chips), 14 (labels, rows, sentences), 16 (group titles, the lead sentence, fact values), 20 (section titles, dock numbers), 32 (the one rate numeral). 18 and 24 go; so do 10,4, 11 and 13. Two weights, 400 and 600. Sentences are never 12 px and never muted when they carry a result.
- **Spacing.** 4 px base; steps 8, 12, 16, 24, 32. Row 44, group header 56, section gap 48.
- **Colour roles.** Ink for values. Muted for labels only. Steel blue means „jetzt" and „interactive". Neutral grey, dashed, means „gespeichert". Green and red only on deltas at or above the threshold. Amber for validation. Category colours only in the Sankey and, with the same hues, in the plan lane.
- **Cards or hairlines.** A card (surface, 1 px border) for the chart and for things that float: popovers, toasts, the drawer. Everything else is rows separated by hairlines. The column has one hairline at its left edge. No dashed boxes, nothing bordered inside something bordered.
- **Motion.** 120 ms for hover and chips, 200 ms for groups and the dock, 280 ms for the chart, 240 ms for numbers, all on `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`. No loops.
- **Width.** From 1680 px the canvas grows to 1.240 px; Sankey and Aufstellung sit side by side.

## R6. Copy principles

1. **Number first, then what it means, in one plain sentence.** „97,4 % – Geld reicht in 97 von 100 Verläufen bis 92."
2. **Every amount carries its unit:** €/Mon., €/Jahr, einmalig. One view, one time base: Entnahme speaks per month everywhere, including the corridor axis and the rule table.
3. **One abbreviation rule.** From one million: „2,21 Mio €". In the head, dock and chips, thousands as „845 T€"; in tables the full figure. No „Mio." with a full stop.
4. **Fixed words** (de; the en file gets the matching set, key parity as today):

   | Use | Not |
   |---|---|
   | Ruhestart | Renteneintrittsalter, Rentenbeginn (for the same field) |
   | Rente ab | Gesetzliches Rentenalter |
   | Planen bis | Planungshorizont, Endalter |
   | mittlerer Verlauf | Median, P50 |
   | schlechter Verlauf (1 von 10) | P10, Schwächere 10 % |
   | Punkte (Pkt.) | pp |
   | Regel | Strategie |
   | gespeichert / jetzt | Basis, Entwurf (in chart legends) |

5. **Say what happens, not what the statistic is called.** „In einem schlechten Verlauf musst du mit 80 auf 3.300 € im Monat kürzen" instead of „Ausgabenspanne 1,97×". The expert terms stay available under „Details".
6. **Buttons name the outcome:** „Als Variante speichern", not „Fertig" where nothing finishes.
7. Informal „du" in the workspace; the PDF keeps „Sie".

## R7. Accessibility constraints

- **Keyboard.** Order: bar → head → canvas anchors → canvas → column. A skip link and F6 jump between canvas and column. The chart is one tab stop; arrow keys move the year cursor, Enter opens Geldfluss for that year. Group headers are buttons with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. Escape in a field restores the value from before the edit. Chart handles (phase 2) are `role="slider"` with arrow keys, Home and End, and each has its stepper twin in the column: nothing is drag-only.
- **Screen reader.** The live sentence that the panel has today („Erfolgsquote jetzt 97,4 %, +1,2 Punkte gegenüber dem gespeicherten Plan") moves to the head and fires once per settled run. The change chips are a list; each ✕ is a button named „Änderung zurücknehmen: Ruhestart 62 → 64". The chart's table equivalent gets a second column „gespeichert".
- **Reduced motion.** No number tween, no chart morph, no dock slide; values swap. The existing rules extend to the new parts.
- **Contrast, light and dark.** The ghost line reaches 3:1 against the chart surface and is dashed and labelled, so it never depends on colour. Delta chips keep their AA pairs. Extend `src/lib/__tests__/contrast.test.ts` with the ghost token and the lane tints, in both dark blocks.
- **Touch.** Every target 44×44 px or larger, 8 px between neighbours; slider thumbs 24 px drawn, 44 px hit area.
- **Zoom and reflow.** At 200 % zoom a 1366 px window falls into the 761–1179 arrangement (dock plus drawer). No sideways scrolling from 320 px up.
